# QCobro Meta ads · Round 1 brief

Decided with Pedro on 2026-09-16, before any campaign settings. Builds on `../../../DIRECTION.md`

## What round 1 tests

**One variable: the image carrier.** Most viewers don't know QCobro, so every ad leads with what it
is. The pilot offer is not the lead message. Three static ads, each shipped as `feed-4x5` +
`vertical-9x16` (`ads.pen` section 07), in one broad ad set:

| Ad  | Image carrier                                          | On-image headline                          | Pill                                              | Primary text first line (≤ 40)         | Headline (≤ 27)      |
| :-- | :----------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------- | :------------------- |
| A   | Real-looking team photo: collector on a call (augment) | SU EQUIPO DE COBROS, AHORA CON **VOZ IA.** | La IA hace las llamadas. Su equipo, los acuerdos. | Su equipo de cobros, ahora con Voz IA. | Cobranzas con Voz IA |
| C   | 3D money art, no person                                | COBRANZAS AUTOMATIZADAS **CON VOZ IA.**    | Llama, escribe y da seguimiento a su cartera      | Cobranzas automatizadas con Voz IA.    | Conozca QCobro       |
| E   | Clearly-AI 3D avatar with headset                      | COBRANZAS AUTOMATIZADAS **CON VOZ IA.**    | Llama y da seguimiento a su cartera               | Cobranzas automatizadas con Voz IA.    | Conozca QCobro       |

C and E share copy, so the image is the only difference between them. A differs in both image and
message by necessity: a photo of a person can't carry an automation claim (see DIRECTION → Look).

Primary text:

- **A:** "Su equipo de cobros, ahora con Voz IA. La IA hace las llamadas; su equipo cierra los acuerdos. QCobro, de Fonoster."
- **C and E:** "Cobranzas automatizadas con Voz IA. QCobro llama, escribe y da seguimiento a su cartera. QCobro, de Fonoster."

CTA: `LEARN_MORE` ("Más información"), because the ad introduces the category and doesn't lead with the pilot.

**AI disclosure:** all three ads use generated imagery (photo, 3D art, avatar) → `OPT_IN`.

## Decisions

| Decision         | Choice                                                               | Why                                                                               |
| :--------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Format           | Static only                                                          | Fastest to produce and judge; the call Reel waits for a real anonymized recording |
| Lead message     | The category ("Cobranzas automatizadas con Voz IA"), not the pilot   | Unaware audience: "going straight to pilot would not work"                        |
| Look             | Scroll-stopping: people, 3D money art, bold type, website v3 colors  | The editorial widget look didn't stop a thumb; colors must match the landing page |
| Headline × image | Human photo ⇒ augment copy; automation copy ⇒ no person or AI avatar | Avoids implying the person in the photo is the AI                                 |
| Concepts dropped | B (manager photo, WhatsApp + SMS), D (paper, WhatsApp + SMS)         | Kept for round 2, where channel is the variable                                   |
| Superseded       | Offer / proof / pain drafts in the editorial style (`ads.pen` §05)   | Rejected in review; kept for reference                                            |

## Competitive context (Ad Library, 2026-09-16)

Almost no collections-software advertisers reach the DR. Kleva runs generic performance copy;
a Peruvian collections system runs feature-list ads. Nothing leads with Voz IA or shows the
collections team, so every concept is uncontested.
