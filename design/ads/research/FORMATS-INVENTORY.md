# Meta ad formats inventory

**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16. **Scope** (from `../DIRECTION.md`): Facebook, Instagram, Messenger,
WhatsApp and Audience Network. Threads is out of scope.

**Sources:**

- **Meta Ads Guide:** 60 spec pages, read as rendered text in Chrome, not through a summarizer.
  Each row cites its page as `AG:<slug>`, meaning
  `https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/<slug>`.
- **Meta Help Center:** articles fetched through the Meta Ads connector, cited as `HC:<id>`, meaning
  `https://www.facebook.com/business/help/<id>`. Raw notes are in `notes-meta-help.md`.

This is research, not the framework. Section 10 lists the decisions it forces. The templates and
guardrails get designed only after this file is reviewed.

The pixel values in parentheses (250/340 px) are relative to a 1080×1920 canvas. At 1440×2560,
14% = 358 px, 20% = 512 px, 35% = 896 px, 40% = 1024 px, and 6% = 86 px.

**Conflict:** 9:16 bottom margins range from 14% to 40% depending on the page. One 9:16 asset
can deliver to all of these placements, so the only safe single rule is the **strictest union:
top 14%, bottom 35% (40% with a disclaimer), sides 6%**. WhatsApp Status inherits that rule by
inference, not from Meta's text.

## 9. Contradictions and gaps to carry forward

1. 9:16 bottom safe zone: 14% vs 20% vs 35% vs 40% (§2). Use the strictest.
2. WhatsApp Status has **no official size or safe-zone spec** in the Ads Guide or Help Center.
3. Stale or copied Ads Guide pages: AN rewarded-video image, FB right-column video, FB Reels
   overlay video (post-loop), AN native image (ratio vs pixels), FB Stories carousel (1:1 at
   1080×1920), IG Explore home carousel (4:5 vs 1:1).
4. IG Feed video: Ads Guide says 9:16; Help Center recommends 4:5 or 9:16.
5. FB Reels image page says both "keep a safe zone for text" and "don't add text on images".
6. Messenger inbox placement is barely documented (only in carousel articles).
7. Meta's recommended _minimums_ still say 1080 in many rows while recommended sizes say 1440.
   Designing at 1440 covers both.
8. Meta's text-overlay pixel geometry (profile row, "Ad" label, CTA bar) isn't published. It can
   only be checked with `ads_get_ad_preview` on a real paused ad.
