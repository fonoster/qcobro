## Carousel — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1114358518575630

- Facebook: 2–10 cards; ratios 1:1, 9:16 (4:5 only for Advantage+ catalog carousel); tolerance 3%;
  1:1 min 500×500, rec 1024×1024 (ads guide says 1080×1080); 9:16 min 500×888, rec 1080×1920. JPG/PNG, ≤30 MB image;
  video up to 240 min (15 s rec), ≤4 GB.
- Messenger & Audience Network: 2–10 cards, 1:1 rec, 1080×1080.
- Instagram: 2–10 cards feed, **max 3 cards in Stories**; 1:1 or 4:5 feed, 9:16 stories; tolerance 1%;
  video ≤2 min feed, ≤15 s stories.
- Copy: primary text 125, headline 40, link description 25 (FB/Messenger/AN). IG text 125, ≤30 hashtags.
- Carousel may be auto-shown as slideshow video; optimizations: cards as video, best card first, end profile card, music.
- Carousel incompatible with "On your ad" and "Facebook page" conversion locations.
